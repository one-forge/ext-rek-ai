=====================
1_FORGE – Rek.ai
=====================

:Extension key:
   one_forge_rekai

:Package name:

:Rendered:
   |today|

----

A TYPO3 extension that integrates the `Rek.ai <https://www.rek.ai/>`_ content recommendation service
into your TYPO3 website. It automatically injects the Rek.ai script into your page headers and provides
a configurable content element for displaying personalised content recommendations.
